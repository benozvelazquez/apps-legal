Standcall Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Standcall plays game and predator calls on your iPhone. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your sequences, your sightings log, your best coach scores and your settings. The coach uses the microphone only while you practice: it scores your call on the phone and never saves or sends the recording. Film uses the camera and microphone only while you record; the videos go to your Photos and are never uploaded. Standcall can add videos to Photos but can't see your other photos.

Anonymous usage stats

Unless you turn them off, Standcall sends anonymous usage events to our server:

  • which screens and calls get used, and when a stand starts, is skipped or finishes
  • paywall views and purchases
  • which version of a screen you saw, when we test two versions against each other
  • the app and iOS version, and the region and language set on the phone

These events are tied to a random number the app makes on your phone (the install id). They are not tied to your name, Apple ID, advertising identifier or location. Our server stores the install id only as a one-way hash, deletes events after 180 days, and doesn't keep IP addresses in its logs.

Feedback you send

If you write to us from More, then Send feedback, we get your message, the kind you picked (problem, sound or idea), and the app and iOS version. It isn't tied to the install id or to anything else about you, so we can't write back. It is sent even with usage stats off, because you chose to send it.

App Store search ads

If you installed Standcall from an App Store search ad and usage stats are on, the app asks Apple once, during the first 7 days, which ad campaign and keyword led to the install. Apple answers with ad ids only, nothing about you. We use it to see which ads are worth paying for. This is not tracking, so there is no tracking prompt.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, sees an anonymous customer id and what was bought. We never see your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Your choices

In the app, open More, then Privacy:

  • Share anonymous usage stats turns the events off.
  • Delete stats sent from this phone removes everything this phone sent from our server.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Who handles the data

Our server runs on Render (Frankfurt, Germany) with a MongoDB Atlas database. Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat. We don't sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Standcall isn't made for children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
