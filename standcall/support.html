Standcall Support

Answers to what hunters ask most. Still stuck? In the app, open More, then Send feedback (anonymous, no reply possible). If you want an answer, write to legendcrafter24@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the Standcall version (at the bottom of the More tab).

I bought Pro, but the app shows it locked

Open More, then Restore purchases (it's also at the bottom of the paywall). There is no account: purchases belong to the Apple ID you bought with, so restore while signed in with that Apple ID. This also brings Pro to a new phone.

Does lifetime stay lifetime?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, and we won't move lifetime buyers to a subscription.

How do I cancel the yearly plan or get a refund?

Cancel in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Standcall. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I use a Bluetooth speaker?

Yes, any Bluetooth speaker, without Pro. Pair it in Settings, then Bluetooth, and play. If the speaker drops out during a stand, Standcall mutes instead of playing from the phone in your pocket and shows a banner with the speaker's name. The calls come back when the speaker reconnects, or tap Play on this phone.

It isn't loud enough

Turn on BOOST on the player bar in the Calls tab: it adds about 8 dB, and a limiter keeps it from distorting. For distance, use a Bluetooth speaker.

Does it keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Calls, loops and whole stands keep playing with the screen locked, and you can pause from the lock screen. It works offline, with no cell signal.

How does the two-phone remote work? (Pro)

Open Standcall on both phones and go to More, then Two-phone remote. On the phone that stays at the decoy, tap This phone is the caller. On the phone in your hand, tap This phone is the remote and connect. The phones talk directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with no cell signal needed. How far it reaches depends on the phones and the terrain, so try it at home before the hunt. If the phones lose each other (or you lock the remote phone), the remote reconnects on its own once they are back in range and its screen is on; the caller keeps playing the stand meanwhile.

Where do the sounds come from?

Wild recordings come from the U.S. National Park Service and from Freesound contributors who released them to the public domain (CC0). Calls marked Original were made for Standcall to sound like the mouth calls hunters blow. More, then Sound credits, lists every sound and its source.

Is it legal to use electronic calls?

It depends on the species and the state. Federal law bans electronic calls for migratory birds such as ducks and geese (light goose conservation orders are a narrow exception), and many states limit them for other game. Check your state's rules before you hunt. More, then Calling rules, has a short guide.

Accessibility

Standcall follows your iPhone's text size (Settings, then Display & Brightness, then Text Size, or Accessibility, then Larger Text). At the largest sizes the call list switches to one column. With Reduce Motion on, the playing bars stop and panels fade instead of sliding. Every theme keeps text readable against its background; Daylight is black on white for bright sun. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us in More, then Send feedback.
