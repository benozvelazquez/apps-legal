Durak Support

Answers to what players ask most. Still stuck? In the app, open Settings, then Feedback (anonymous, no reply possible). If you want an answer, write to legendcrafter24@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the Durak version (at the bottom of Settings).

I bought Durak Pro, but the app doesn't show it

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (it's also at the bottom of the paywall). There is no account: purchases belong to the Apple ID you bought with, so restore while signed in with that Apple ID. This also brings Durak Pro to a new phone.

Does lifetime stay lifetime?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, and we won't move lifetime buyers to a subscription.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Cancel in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Durak. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which rules does Durak use?

Podkidnoy (throw-in) and Perevodnoy (transfer), with 24, 36 or 52 cards and 2 to 6 players. You choose whether everyone may throw in or only the attacker, and whether the first attack is limited to five cards. Settings, then How to play, explains every rule.

What is Shuler?

An optional table rule where cheating is allowed. You may drag a card that doesn't beat, or throw in a rank that isn't on the table, and hope nobody notices. If you spot a cheat, tap Caught! at the top: the cheater takes every card on the table. A wrong call costs one of your three calls for the game. The computer only cheats when Shuler is on, and it can be caught too. Shuler is off for online games and games on one phone.

Is the computer cheating when Shuler is off?

No. Every shuffle is random and the computer plays only from what it can see: its own hand, the table and the cards that have gone out. It doesn't look at your hand or the deck.

How does the daily deal work?

Everyone gets the same cards against the same two rivals that day. Only your first try counts for the streak and the leaderboard. A new deal starts at midnight, phone time. Durak Pro can replay the last 30 deals; replays don't change the streak.

What is tea for?

Tea is for the night train journey. Losing a station (or leaving one mid-game) costs a glass, one glass refills every 20 minutes, and three stars at a station pours an extra one. For some players, losing a quick game against a Hard opponent costs a glass too. Durak Pro gives unlimited tea. The daily deal and games with friends on one phone, nearby or online never use tea.

How do I play with friends?

On one phone: start a Table game, put the phone flat between you, and each player peeks at their own hand when it's their turn. Nearby or online: sign in to Game Center (iPhone Settings, then Game Center), then tap Nearby or Online on the home screen. Online lets you invite Game Center friends or meet new players.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything except online games, leaderboards and achievements works without a connection. Stats you've allowed are sent later.

Accessibility

VoiceOver reads every card, the trump, how many cards are left in the deck and how many each player holds. With Reduce Motion on, the landscape outside the train window stays still. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us in Settings, then Feedback.
