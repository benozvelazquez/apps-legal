Durak Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Durak is a card game for iPhone. It has no account. Ads appear only between games, and only for people who already play regularly; Durak Pro removes them.

What stays on your phone

Your journey progress and stars, daily deal results and streaks, statistics, tea, the card backs and tablecloths you picked, your rule settings and any game in progress. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Anonymous usage stats

Unless you turn them off, Durak sends anonymous usage events to our server:

  • which rules and modes get played, how games end, and where games get too hard
  • paywall views and purchases
  • which version of a screen you saw, when we test two versions against each other
  • the app and iOS version, and the region and language set on the phone

These events are tied to a random number the app makes on your phone (the install id). They are not tied to your name, Apple ID, Game Center account, advertising identifier or location. Our server stores the install id only as a one-way hash, deletes events after 180 days, and doesn't keep IP addresses in its logs.

iCloud backup

Your statistics, journey stars, daily deal results and earned achievements are also saved in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage), so they come back on a new phone. We can't see it. Turn it off in Settings, then Back up progress to iCloud.

Ads

New and occasional players never see ads. Once you play often (for example on several days of a week), an ad may show after a finished game, when you tap Play again, Next or Back: never during a game, never in table mode or online games, and never with Durak Pro. You can also choose to watch an ad for a glass of tea.

Ads come from Google AdMob. Before any ad loads, players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form, and iOS asks whether Durak may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to choose ads; if you don't, it can't, and the ads are less personalized. Google may also receive your IP address, rough location from it and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and, where it applies, in Durak's Settings, then Ad privacy choices.

The app logs, with the anonymous usage stats above, how often you play and whether an ad was shown at a break, so we can keep ads rare. That record uses the random install id, not the advertising identifier.

Feedback you send

If you write to us from Settings, then Feedback, we get your message, the app and iOS version and the phone's language. It isn't tied to the install id or to anything else about you, so we can't write back. It is sent even with usage stats off, because you chose to send it.

Game Center

Online and nearby games, leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center, under Apple's privacy policy. During an online game, the moves of that game pass between the players' phones through Game Center. Durak doesn't send your Game Center name or id to our server.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, sees an anonymous customer id and what was bought. We never see your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Your choices

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy:

  • Share anonymous usage stats turns the events off.
  • Delete what was sent removes everything this phone sent from our server and starts over with a new install id.
  • Ad privacy choices (where Google's consent applies) reopens the consent form.

Who handles the data

Our server runs on Render (Frankfurt, Germany) with a MongoDB Atlas database. Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat, multiplayer through Apple Game Center, ads through Google AdMob. We don't sell our usage stats or share them with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Durak is not directed at children and asks no one for personal information. Ads are requested as general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
